Location

The MultiComp Lab is affiliated with Language Technologies Institute in School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University. We are located on the 5th floor of Gates and Hillman Complex (GHC) which can be found with the address 4902 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15213, USA

The closest airport is the Pittsburgh International Airport. If you are looking for a short-term stay in Pittsburgh close to CMU we would recommend Wyndham Pittsburgh University Center or Shadyside Inn.
