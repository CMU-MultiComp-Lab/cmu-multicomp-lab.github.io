## Location

The MultiComp Lab is affiliated with [Language Technologies Institute](http://lti.cs.cmu.edu/) in [School of Computer Science](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/), [Carnegie Mellon University](http://www.cmu.edu/). We are located on the 5th floor of Gates and Hillman Complex (GHC) which can be found with the address *4902 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15213, USA*

The closest airport is the [Pittsburgh International Airport](https://goo.gl/maps/yNdLmBXYaVT2). If you are looking for a short-term stay in Pittsburgh close to CMU we would recommend *Wyndham Pittsburgh University Center*or *Shadyside Inn.*
