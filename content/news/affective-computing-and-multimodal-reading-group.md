It is with immense pleasure that we announce a new reading group on Affective Computing and Multimodal. This reading group will focus on recent papers that study human communicative behaviors, emotional state and behavioral traits. We will read recently published work in venues such as IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, ACII, ICMI, HRI and UbiComp.

This reading group will follow a round-table format where each person will share their viewpoints, followed by an in-depth discussion of points that were misunderstood. You cannot attend the reading group if you did not read the paper!
