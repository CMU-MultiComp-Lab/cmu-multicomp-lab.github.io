Dr. Louis-Philippe Morency of the [Language Technologies Institute](https://www.lti.cs.cmu.edu/) received a three-year Finmeccanica Career Development Professorship in Computer Science during a Feb. 19 ceremony.

[Morency](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~morency/) joined the LTI in 2015 as an assistant professor and director of the [Multimodal Communication and Machine Learning Laboratory](/). His research focuses on enabling computers to analyze, recognize and predict subtle human communicative behaviors, such as facial expressions, during social interactions.

Click [here](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/news/morency-receives-finmeccanica-chair) for the announcement.
