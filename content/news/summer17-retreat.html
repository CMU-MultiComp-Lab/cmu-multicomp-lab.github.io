Multicomp had its summer retreat in the last week of June where all of us scooted off to a cabin in the woods for a change pace and scenery. With delicious meals prepared by our very own students, we spent a part of the retreat brainstorming about the research directions for the academic year 2017-18.

For the last part of the trip we visited an architectural marvel known as “Falling Water”
