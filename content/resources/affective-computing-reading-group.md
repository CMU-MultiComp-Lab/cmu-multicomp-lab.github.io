This reading group focuses on papers that study human communicative behaviors, affective states, and behavioral traits. We read recent and seminal published work in venues such as IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, ACII, ICMI, HRI and UbiComp. Below is a meeting schedule and assigned readings.

##### Spring 2019

- **February 13** (Jeff Girard)
  Bandini et al. (2018). Automatic detection of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) from video-based analysis of facial movements: Speech and non-speech tasks. *Proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on Automatic Face and Gesture Recognition*, 150–157. <https://doi.org/10/gfrkjk>
- **February 27** (Alex Vail)
  Jaques, N., McDuff, D., Kim, Y. L., & Picard, R. (2016). Understanding and predicting bonding in conversations using thin slices of facial expressions and body language. *Proceedings of the International Conference on Intelligent Virtual Agents,* 64–74. <https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-47665-0_6>
- **March 27** (Torsten Wörtwein)
  Khorram, S., Gideon, J., McInnis, M., & Provost, E. M. (2016). *Recognition of depression in bipolar disorder: Leveraging cohort and person-specific knowledge*. 1215–1219. <https://doi.org/10/gfxh3z>
  Zhang, B., Essl, G., & Mower Provost, E. (2017). Predicting the distribution of emotion perception: capturing inter-rater variability. *Proceedings of the ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction*, 51–59. <https://doi.org/10/gfxh32>
- **April 24** (Lujie Chen)
  Hamaker, E. L., Ceulemans, E., Grasman, R. P. P. P., & Tuerlinckx, F. (2015). Modeling affect dynamics: State of the art and future challenges. *Emotion Review*, *7*(4), 316–322. <https://doi.org/10/gfx6zb>

##### Spring 2018

Tian, Leimin, et al. “Recognizing Induced Emotions of Movie Audiences: Are Induced and Perceived Emotions the Same?.” ACII

##### Fall 2017

Martinez, Aleix M. “Visual perception of facial expressions of emotion.” Current opinion in psychology

Marsella, Stacy C., and Jonathan Gratch. “EMA: A process model of appraisal dynamics.” Cognitive Systems Research

Zhang, Biqiao, Georg Essl, and Emily Mower Provost. “Automatic recognition of self-reported and perceived emotion: does joint modeling help?.” Proceedings of the 18th ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction

Guillame-Bert, Mathieu, and James L. Crowley. “Learning temporal association rules on symbolic time sequences.” Asian conference on machine learning

Lei, Yinjie, Mohammed Bennamoun, and Amar A. El-Sallam. “An efficient 3D face recognition approach based on the fusion of novel local low-level features.” Pattern Recognition

Girard, Jeffrey M., and Jeffrey F. Cohn. “A primer on observational measurement.” Assessment
