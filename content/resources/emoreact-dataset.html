Description

The EmoReact Dataset is a newly collected multimodal emotion dataset of children between the ages of four and fourteen years old. The dataset contains 1102 audio-visual clips annotated for 17 different emotional states: six basic emotions, neutral, valence and nine complex emotions including curiosity, uncertainty and frustration.

Publications

B. Nojavanasghari, T. Baltrušaitis, C. Hughes and L.-P. Morency. Emoreact: A Multimodal Approach and Dataset for Recognizing Emotional Responses in Children. In Proceedings of the ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction (ICMI), 2016
