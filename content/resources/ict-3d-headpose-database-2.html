Description

The database contains 10 sequences acquired with a Microsoft Kinect sensor; 6 participants were male 4 female.
Participants were recorded while turning their heads, sitting in front of the sensor.
The ground truth head pose was collected using the Polhemus Fastrack flock of birds tracker attached to a cap the participants were wearing.

For more information on the use of this database please visit the 3D Constrained Local Model for Rigid and Non-Rigid Facial Tracking project page.
