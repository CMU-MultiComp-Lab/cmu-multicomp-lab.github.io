## Description

The Multi-Modal Movie Opinion (ICT-MMMO) dataset consists of online social review videos that encompass a strong diversity in how people express opinions. The dataset contains 343 multimodal review videos annotated at the video level for sentiment. In addition to textual information, audio features as typically used in speech-based emotion recognition as well as video features encoding valuable valence information conveyed by the speaker are also included.

ICT-MMMO dataset is available through [CMU Multimodal Data SDK on GitHub: https://github.com/CMU-MultiComp-Lab/CMU-MultimodalSDK](https://github.com/CMU-MultiComp-Lab/CMU-MultimodalSDK)
