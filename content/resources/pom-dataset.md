## Description

The Persuasive Opinion Multimedia (POM) corpus consists of 1,000 movie review videos obtained from a social multimedia website called ExpoTV.com. These videos are annotated for multiple speaker traits including confidence, passion, dominance, credibility, entertaining, reserved, trusting, relaxed, nervous, humorous and persuasiveness.

## Publications

S. Park, M. Chatterjee, H. S. Shim, K. Sagea and L.-P. Morency, [Computational Analysis of Persuasiveness in Social Multimedia: A Novel Dataset and Multimodal Prediction Approach](http://dx.doi.org/10.1145/2663204.2663260). In *Proceedings of the ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction* (***ICMI***), 2014
